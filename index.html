Queensland Government

9 Nov 2026 9:00 AM

Hi

The Service Queensland app is here and has now replaced the Digital Licence app.

Your existing digital licences and cards are now stored and available in the new Service Queensland app. You can also access a range of online services from the Service Queensland app - with more cards and services on the way!

Service Queensland - a new, more connected way to access Queensland Government services online, over the phone, and in person.
